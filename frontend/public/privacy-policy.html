Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

1. Introduction

CyberGuard ("we", "our", "us") is a cyberbullying detection system that monitors and analyzes text content from social media platforms to identify harmful content. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our service.

2. Information We Collect

When you connect your Facebook Page or Instagram Account to CyberGuard, we collect:

  • Comment Text: The text content of comments made on your connected social media posts
  • Comment Author: The username or display name of the person who posted the comment
  • Platform Metadata: The platform source (Facebook or Instagram), comment timestamp, and post identifier
  • Account Information: Your Facebook/Instagram page or account ID used for webhook subscription

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Analyzing comment text for cyberbullying, hate speech, harassment, threats, and religiously sensitive content
  • Displaying flagged content in the admin dashboard for moderation purposes
  • Providing toxicity scores and severity classifications
  • Enabling moderation actions (warn, block, delete) through the admin panel

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored securely in our database with access controls
  • Only authenticated administrators can access flagged content and moderation tools
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties
  • Data is retained for the duration necessary to provide the moderation service

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties except:

  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect the rights and safety of our users

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we have collected about your connected accounts
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Disconnect your social media accounts from our service at any time
  • Request a copy of your data

7. Meta Platform Data

In compliance with Meta's Platform Terms:

  • We only access data that is necessary for cyberbullying detection and moderation
  • We do not use Meta platform data for advertising or marketing purposes
  • We do not transfer Meta platform data to data brokers or information resellers
  • We delete Meta platform data when it is no longer needed for the moderation service

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
